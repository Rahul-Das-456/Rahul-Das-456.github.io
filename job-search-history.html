The volume play

2017 to 2019, 4,000 applicationsFresh out of undergrad, with no compliance brand and no network. I solved it the only way that felt controllable: apply everywhere, every day. It was exhausting and humbling, and it worked, but the math was brutal.

Two years later, a different strategy.

The targeted play

2023 to 2024, 300 applicationsThis time: SIE certified, CAPM in hand, four years of compliance experience and a deliberate list of target firms. I applied only where I had a referral, a direct recruiter connection or a strong profile match. 93% fewer applications, the same number accepted.

Volume against targeting

The ghosted rate fell from 90% to 50% just by narrowing scope. A referral or a recruiter relationship all but ended the disappearing into an applicant tracking system.

The shape of the funnel

The first search was wide at the top and nearly empty by the final rounds. The second held steady conversion at every stage, a sign of better fit rather than better luck.

Same result, a fraction of the cost

Both searches ended with three accepted roles. The second took 13× fewer applications: 3,700 fewer rejections, fewer months and far less strain.

What it says about compliance hiring

Compliance hiring runs on relationships. Volume could not make up for missing credentials in the first search; credentials and targeted positioning did in the second.
